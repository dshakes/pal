1. Gateway:    kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pal.fullname" . }}-gateway 8443:8443
2. Dashboard:  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pal.fullname" . }}-dashboard 3001:3000
3. Control Plane (gRPC): kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "pal.fullname" . }}-control-plane 50051:50051

To get started:
  pal init my-agent
  pal deploy
  pal run my-agent --input '{"message": "hello"}'
